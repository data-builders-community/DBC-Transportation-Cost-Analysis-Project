# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. Who can participate in this project?

Anyone interested in gaining practical experience in data analytics through a real-world collaborative project is welcome to participate.

The project is particularly suited to aspiring data analysts, students, career changers, and self-taught learners who want to strengthen their portfolio while collaborating with others.

---

## 2. Do I need to be an experienced data analyst?

No. Participants are only expected to have a basic understanding of data analysis and Microsoft Excel.

The project is designed to help participants learn by applying their skills to a real-world problem.

---

## 3. Which tools can I use?

You are free to use any data analysis or visualization tool you are comfortable with.

Examples include:

- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI
- Tableau
- Python
- Looker Studio

The focus of the project is on the quality of the analysis and insights rather than the software used.

---

## 4. What destinations can I choose?

Choose one destination category that is relevant to your city.

Examples include:

- Universities
- Major Markets
- Government Hospitals
- Business Districts

Within your chosen category, you may select two or three major destinations for your analysis.

---

## 5. How many residential areas should I include?

Participants should select approximately **10–15 residential areas** that provide good coverage across their city.

Residential areas should be represented by well-known bus stops, junctions, or landmarks.

---

## 6. What is the difference between a Direct route and a Multi-Stops route?

**Direct**

The commuter travels from the starting point to the destination without changing vehicles.

**Multi-Stops**

The commuter changes vehicles one or more times before reaching the final destination.

---

## 7. Do I only record the total transportation fare?

No.

Participants should record the fare paid for **each stage of the journey**.

The total transportation fare is calculated from all stages of the journey.

---

## 8. Do I have to collect data from registered motor parks?

No.

Transportation fares may be verified using drivers, conductors, or regular commuters.

However, residential areas should be identified using major bus stops, junctions, or landmarks to ensure consistency.

---

## 9. How can I ensure my data is accurate?

Whenever possible:

- Verify fares with drivers or regular commuters.
- Avoid estimating transportation costs.
- Review your entries before submitting your dataset.

---

## 10. Where should I ask project-related questions?

Please ask project-related questions in the project's WhatsApp community.

This allows everyone to learn from the discussion, contribute ideas, and help improve the project together.

---

## 11. What will I have when I complete the project?

By the end of the project, you will have:

- An original transportation dataset collected from your city.
- A cleaned dataset ready for analysis.
- An interactive dashboard.
- A project report.
- A portfolio-ready data analytics project.
- A contribution to the collaborative multi-city dataset.

---

## 12. What happens after I complete my project?

Once participants complete their individual city projects, the datasets will be combined into a larger collaborative dataset.

This allows participants to perform comparative analyses across multiple cities and uncover transportation patterns that would not be visible from a single city's data.
